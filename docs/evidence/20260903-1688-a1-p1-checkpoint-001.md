## Implemented boundary

Schema v41 adds the additive `connector_sync_checkpoints` table. Its identity is `tenant_id + connector_id + store_id + resource + window_kind + window_start + window_end`. Full runs page until exhaustion and reconcile stable `totalRecords`; incremental runs keep their window total out of `upstream_total` and write a completion watermark. Successful page application and checkpoint advancement share one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction. The lease and `row_version` provide cross-process ownership and stale-writer rejection; an expired lease can be recovered.
